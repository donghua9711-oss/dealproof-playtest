How the grades work. Every price was read at the named store on Friday 27 November 2026 and is stamped with its check time and source. Verified new low is the lowest price tracked for that product; strong price is a deep, genuine cut; routine sale is real but repeats through the year; inflated anchor means the “usual” price was freshly hiked or is a store-brand anchor, so the headline percent flatters the deal. The board is ordered by a score combining the size of the cut, the dollars saved and that grade, so the best deals come first. Store sale windows: Best Buy ends 28 Nov, Walmart and Target end 29 Nov, Amazon ends 30 Nov (the stores' Black Friday releases and DealNews, 27 Nov).
